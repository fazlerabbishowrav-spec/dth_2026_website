# Which open source project would be useful in your major, and why?

## Zotero

Zotero is an open-source tool for collecting and organising academic sources. It helps users save articles, manage references and create citations. The project was created at George Mason University and is now developed by Digital Scholar with support from a wider community.

How can it help my major?

European Studies includes topics such as migration, EU policies, human rights and international relations. Students often work with many academic articles and reports, which can be difficult to organise. Zotero can make this work easier. During my thesis research on migrant labour market integration in Denmark and Sweden, I can use folders for different topics, save useful articles and add notes. It also helps create citations and reference lists, which saves time during academic writing.

People: Zotero has 90 contributors.

Questions: There are 1,410 open issues and 2,691 closed issues. This suggests that contributors report problems and that many issues have been addressed.

Money: Zotero receives income from individual and institutional storage subscriptions. This helps cover development and operating costs.


My opinion

I would use Zotero for my thesis because it makes academic research easier to organise. It also helps me manage references and avoid mistakes in citations. I would still keep backup copies of important documents. If the original developers stopped working on Zotero, other developers could continue the project because its source code is available. However, its future would depend on people who are willing to maintain it.
